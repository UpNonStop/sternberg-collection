From Ohad’s notebook

The Architecture Series is the one I point people to when they say a coffee mug cannot be design. Each city gets reduced to a single continuous skyline drawn in one weight of line, no color, no clutter, just the profile of a place. Los Angeles, Chicago, St. Louis with its arch, Pike Place, Niagara. Put six of them in a row and you are looking at a typeface for cities.

There is a matching run of demitasse minis too, the same drawings shrunk onto espresso cups, and they are the hardest ones to find complete. We have the series running deep here, one of the largest single runs in the whole archive at a hundred and sixty pieces.
