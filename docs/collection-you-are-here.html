From Ohad’s notebook

If the Architecture Series is the quiet one, You Are Here is the loud one, and it is the series most people actually recognize. It started in 2013 as the new flagship travel line, a fat fourteen ounce mug crammed edge to edge with the icons of a place. The Texas one has the boots and the oil derrick, Hawaii has the surfer and the turtle, Animal Kingdom has the tree of life. Bright, busy, unapologetic.

What makes it a collector trap is that Starbucks treats it as living inventory, retiring designs and slipping in new ones, so chasing a complete set is like chasing a moving train. We are running it well past a hundred places here, the spine of the whole modern era of the collection.
