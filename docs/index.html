“The Holy Grail of Starbucks collectibles.”

The Giant Abbey

A Few of the Stories

Pieces that would make a collection on their own.

01
Where it all began

The 1994 Originals

The very first Starbucks City Mug series ever produced, the year Starbucks collecting itself began. The Minneapolis and St. Paul 1994 is the one people chase, an early city mug that has quietly traded for around two thousand dollars.

02
A complete set of giants

The 2002 Oversized GIANTS

Twenty-four ounce ceramic display pieces released, in the company’s own words, to only the highest-volume stores. The Pike Place version is the most sought after. The collection holds all sixteen cities, the complete set.

03
A Pulitzer winner’s coffee gag

Doonesbury × Garry Trudeau

Designed for Starbucks by the Pulitzer-winning cartoonist, each piece numbered out of 1,500, including the Java Pump statue at number 47, an exceptionally low number in the run.

04
A country that treats it as craft

The Japan corpus

Twenty-three separate sub-series under one country, from the worldwide Global Icon cities to the JIMOTO regional line made in real Kutani porcelain. Over a hundred Japan pieces, the deepest national section in the whole archive.

In Memory

Chaim Sternberg spent fifty-three years quietly building what no museum and no other private collector on record ever has. When he passed on the 29th of February, 2024, his son Ohad set out to honor him the only way that felt right: by documenting every last piece, so the whole of it could be seen at once.In loving memory of Chaim Sternberg, 1940 to 2024
