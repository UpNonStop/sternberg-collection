The archive

What it is

Across fifty-three years, one collector documented a global brand's evolution piece by piece. The Sternberg Collection now stands as a complete material record — not a curated selection, but the full inventory: every Architecture Demitasse, every Charity Edition city, every Doonesbury limited edition, every State of Bean — assembled, catalogued, photographed, and preserved.

The collection includes 45+ verified cities in the Architecture Series, the complete 16-of-16 Charity Edition cities, the full 10-of-10 Doonesbury limited edition set, 14+ Skyline GIANTS confirmed via bottom-stamp OCR, and 12+ verified states in the Daleet Zigelboim States of Bean series — alongside the anchor Giant Abbey at 138oz, the largest production Starbucks vessel ever made.

Every piece has a value, every value has comp evidence, and every inventory line is cross-referenced to box location, photo file, and series. See the verification framework →
