I. The Collector

Chaim Sternberg spent fifty-three years quietly building what no museum, no corporation, and no other private individual on record has ever assembled.

He passed on the 29th of February, 2024, a date that exists once every four years.

In the weeks that followed, his son Ohad committed to honoring him through the work itself: eighteen days, two thousand four hundred and sixty-eight items, each individually ticketed, each photographed from four angles.

“The cataloging is now its own artifact.”

The closest known comparable private holding is an Austrian collector with 409 You Are Here mugs. The Sternberg Collection is six times larger, spans every major series Starbucks has ever produced, and includes pieces from markets the company has since abandoned.

IV. The Headline Pieces

Beyond the anchor, a handful of pieces that justify the collection on their own.

01
The Banned Mug

Bahrain v1, Pearl Monument

It depicted the Pearl Roundabout, demolished by the Bahraini government in 2011 during the Arab Spring, after which all depictions were declared illegal and production halted. One of the rarest pieces in the entire Starbucks universe. The Sternberg Collection contains three.
