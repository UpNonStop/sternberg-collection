Skyline Series One Oversized

The GIANTS

Oversized city skyline vessels, among the rarest pieces in the Starbucks archive.

22 oz  ·  16 cities

Scroll

Sixteen cities. One regional variant. The largest vessels Starbucks ever put into production.

In 2002, Starbucks produced the Skyline Series One Oversized collection, 22-ounce ceramic vessels featuring the silhouettes and architectural skylines of sixteen major North American cities.

The series was distributed exclusively through flagship Starbucks locations in each respective city, plus select highest-volume corporate channels. Original retail was $49.95 USD per piece, five times the price of the standard Skyline mug. They were never high-volume retail items: a manufacturing exercise in vessel scale, placed only where they would be seen by the right audience.

Most extant examples have been used as display objects, scratched in transit, or broken outright. Surviving NIB examples represent a small fraction of original production. Completing the full 16-city set has historically required specialized brokers and years of patience, the geographic distribution model made assembly functionally impossible for any single collector without deliberate effort across multiple markets.

“A complete set was always geographically impossible to assemble.”

The Sternberg Collection holds 13 cities confirmed via bottom-stamp OCR, SKU numbers, copyright year, and original retail markings all intact. Plus the Tahoe regional variant, extending the series beyond the pure-city framework. Three cities (Chicago, Miami, San Francisco) are documented in inventory, pending Tier A visual inspection.

Why This Series Matters
01
Geographic Rarity

Distribution Tied to Flagship Cities

Each GIANT was sold only in its respective city’s flagship Starbucks and select highest-volume corporate channels. No mail-order. No secondary distribution. To acquire all sixteen required physical presence in sixteen cities across the US and Canada, or a network of local buyers. This structural scarcity is permanent and unrepeatable.

02
Brand History

Starbucks’ Geographic Identity, 2002

The cities chosen document exactly where Starbucks saw itself at a specific historical moment: post-IPO expansion, pre-international-collector-era. The sixteen cities are the company’s flagship US and Canadian markets of that period. The series is a primary-source artifact of corporate geography, more legible and durable than any annual report.

03
Scale & Format

The Largest Vessel in the Skyline Family

The GIANTS are the 22-oz counterpart to the 18-oz standard Skyline mugs (original retail $9.95–$12.95). At more than five times the original retail price, they were designed as display objects first. The ceramic weight and skyline relief detail at this scale are qualitatively different from the standard-format piece. Surviving NIB examples retain that scale intact.

04
Collection Anchor

Most Comprehensive Documented Set in Private Hands

13 cities OCR-confirmed + 3 pending Tier A visual inspection + the Tahoe regional variant = 17 pieces, making this substantially the most complete documented GIANT series held by a private collector. Once the three pending cities are visually confirmed, the collection exceeds the originally documented 16-city framework entirely.
