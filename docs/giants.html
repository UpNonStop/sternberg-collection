In 2002, Starbucks produced the Skyline Series One Oversized collection — 22-ounce ceramic vessels featuring the silhouettes and architectural skylines of sixteen major North American cities. The series was distributed through flagship Starbucks stores in each respective city, plus select corporate channels. Original retail was $49.95 USD per piece.

The Skyline GIANTS were never high-volume retail items. They were a manufacturing exercise in vessel scale, distributed in limited quantities. Most extant examples have been used as decorative vessels, scratched in transit, or broken. Surviving NIB examples represent a small fraction of original production.

The Sternberg Collection holds 13 city GIANTS confirmed via bottom-stamp OCR, with bottom-stamp evidence including SKU numbers, copyright year, and original $49.95 USA retail markings. The collection additionally includes a Tahoe Skyline GIANT (SKU 183158) — a regional variant that extends the series beyond pure cities, confirming the GIANT series is larger than the original 16-city framework suggested.

Three city GIANTS — Chicago, Miami, San Francisco — are documented in the inventory but their bottom stamps have not yet surfaced through Drive OCR. They are pending Tier A visual inspection. Once confirmed, the collection's GIANT count exceeds 17 pieces, making this the most comprehensive documented GIANT series in private hands.

Why this series matters

The Skyline GIANTS sit in a particular niche: they are the largest piece of the Skyline collection family (the same series produced 18oz "Regular" mugs at $9.95-$12.95 retail), they are oversized enough to function as display objects rather than drinkware, and they were tied to specific flagship-city distribution — meaning a complete set was always geographically hard to assemble.

For a brand-history archive, the GIANT series documents Starbucks' geographic identity at a specific moment: 2002, post-1996 IPO expansion phase, before international skylines became the dominant collector theme. The cities chosen were the company's flagship US and Canadian markets. The series tells the story of "where Starbucks saw itself" at that moment.

For a private collector, completing the 16-city set has been functionally impossible without specialized brokers — making a substantially complete intact set (14 OCR-verified + 3 pending + Tahoe regional) one of the strongest series anchors in the Sternberg Collection.
