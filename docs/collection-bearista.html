From Ohad’s notebook

Everyone has a soft spot for the Bearistas, and I am no exception. Starbucks has been dressing these little plush bears up since the late nineties, a new costume for every season and city: the Statue of Liberty in New York, kimonos in Japan, Easter rabbits, MLB uniforms, Chinese New Year.

I have over two hundred of them. Lined up together they are basically a parade of everywhere Starbucks has ever been, and almost every one still wears its red tag.
