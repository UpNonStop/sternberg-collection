From Ohad’s notebook

If you want to understand how deep this collection runs, look at Japan. It is not a handful of city mugs, it is twenty-three separate sub-series under one country: the Global Icon cities, the JIMOTO regional line made in real Kutani porcelain, the Roppongi Hills anniversary mugs, the cherry-blossom Reserve pieces, snow globes, card sets.

Over a hundred Japan items in all. Japan treats Starbucks merchandise as a craft, and you can feel it in the weight of every piece.
