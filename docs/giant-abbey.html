The Giant Abbey is the largest production Starbucks vessel ever manufactured. At 138 ounces of capacity and 15 pounds of weight, it exists at the outer edge of what the brand has ever produced — a piece that sits between ceramic vessel, decorative object, and brand statement.

The Sternberg Collection's Giant Abbey is preserved in its original packaging (New In Box), photographed from all angles, and documented with full provenance. It has never been used. It has never been displayed. It has sat preserved as the centerpiece of the archive since its acquisition.

For any institutional buyer of the lot, the Giant Abbey is the singular anchor — the piece that pulls the entire archive into focus and gives the collection its visual gravity.

Why it matters

The Giant Abbey was never a high-volume retail item. It was an outlier — a manufacturing exercise in scale, distributed in limited quantities to flagship stores and corporate channels. Most extant examples have been used, scratched, or broken. NIB survivors are a vanishingly small fraction of original production.

Standalone collector value is documented in comparable sales at $2,200-$2,900. With the intact-collection premium that comes from being part of the larger Sternberg archive, the defensible high value sits at $3,500-$3,800 — not as a separate sale, but as the anchor component of the whole lot.

Documentation

The Giant Abbey is one of 173 photo-verified pieces in the collection. Its bottom stamp, SKU 168515, has been confirmed through both visual inspection and Drive-indexed OCR. The original box, manufacturing markings, and copyright stamps are intact and visible.
