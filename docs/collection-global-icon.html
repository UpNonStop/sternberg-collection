The Global Icon Series
The line that went everywhere.
The worldwide city series that succeeded the Collector Series, and the single largest category in the entire archive.

From Ohad’s notebook

When Starbucks retired the old Collector Series around 2013, they replaced it with this: the Global Icon line, a single worldwide template carrying each city’s landmarks in a tidy band of icons. Daegu, Cusco, Katowice, Phuket, St. Petersburg, Oktoberfest. It is the series that finally let the brand mug every city on earth on one consistent design.

It is also the largest single category we hold, which makes sense, because it is still in production and still expanding. The collectors I know treat Global Icon as the endgame, the series you never actually finish. That is exactly why a deep run of it matters; depth here is the rarest thing of all.
