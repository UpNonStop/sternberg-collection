The Long Tail
A thousand more, and still counting.
The everyday City Mug line and the pieces that belong to no single series. The widest, deepest part of the whole archive.

From Ohad’s notebook

Every great collection has a long tail, and ours is enormous. Past the famous series sits the plain City Mug line, the simple bold-name cups that Starbucks made for what feels like every airport and high street on earth. Boston, Brisbane, Bangkok, Singapore, Wellington, Melbourne, Chengdu, Jeju. On their own each is modest; together they are a world map a thousand cups wide.

This is the part collectors underestimate and museums love, because completeness lives here. Anyone can chase the headline rarities. Holding the long tail, the unglamorous everyday cups in this kind of depth, is what turns a pile of mugs into an archive. There are over a thousand pieces in this bucket alone.
