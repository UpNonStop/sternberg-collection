The Collector Series
The spine of the whole archive.
Three hundred and twenty five destinations on the classic Collector Series silhouette. If one series defines this collection, it is this one.

From Ohad’s notebook

Every serious Starbucks collection is measured against the Collector Series, and this is where ours goes deepest, three hundred and twenty five cities and countries on the same dependable shape. It is the series that taught me geography I never learned in school. Qatar, Oman, Jordan, the Sultanate, Saudi Arabia, a whole Gulf shelf that almost never comes up complete on the secondary market.

People always ask about Bahrain, because there is a famous early Bahrain variant collectors whisper about. The one we hold is the standard Collector Series Bahrain, and honestly it is rare enough on its own; I have watched the Gulf states sit at the top of want lists for years. The joy of this series is not any single mug, it is the wall of them, three hundred places that read like a passport.
