The Collector

Chaim Sternberg

1944–2024

He passed on the 29th of February, 2024, a date that exists once every four years.

Scroll

“A serious collection is never about quantity. It is about a record, the discipline of saying yes to one more piece, every year, for fifty-three years.”

Chaim Sternberg began collecting Starbucks memorabilia in the early 1970s, when Starbucks was a small Seattle storefront and the company’s mugs were unremarkable household objects.

Over the next five decades, what began as casual acquisition became a disciplined archive, box by box, series by series, each piece numbered, catalogued, and photographed.

His approach was methodical. Every piece was assigned a ticket number. Every box was color-coded. Every series was tracked for completion. Where Starbucks released a charity edition tied to a specific city, Chaim acquired all sixteen. Where Garry Trudeau illustrated a limited Doonesbury set, Chaim acquired all ten. Where the brand released oversized Skyline GIANT vessels, Chaim found thirteen of sixteen.

By the time he passed in 2024, the collection had grown to 2,468 individual pieces across 89 storage boxes, 106 distinct series, and 9,872 reference photographs. Chaim maintained a public Instagram archive at @starbucks.mug.collection, 2,466 posts, documenting each acquisition with care and quiet enthusiasm.

What Chaim built is not a curated selection. It is the complete record. The collection now stands as the largest documented private archive of Starbucks brand memorabilia in existence.

The collection is now in the custody of his family, who have undertaken to preserve it as a single intact lot. The decision to offer it to institutional buyers reflects Chaim’s own conviction, that this archive only retains its meaning when kept whole.
