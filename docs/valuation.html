What makes this defensible

Every item has a number. No gaps in coverage. The seller can answer "what's it worth" for any of 2,468 inventory lines, anywhere in the collection, on demand.

Three bands per item. Low, mid, high. Conservative through aggressive. Each anchored to identifiable comp sources — eBay Sold, Heritage Auctions realized, specialty dealer asking prices, Replacements.com.

Series-completion premiums where defensible. Intact 16/16 Charity Edition gets a 30% premium. Complete 10/10 Doonesbury LE gets a 50% premium. 12-of-50 States of Bean gets a partial premium scaled to completion. The mechanics are transparent.

Premium build-up is multiplicative on the lot, not on pieces. This is how auction houses actually value intact archives. Each step is a documented premium type with auction-house precedent.

Risk-adjusted downside is published. The seller is transparent about comp-tightening risk. A buyer sees the floor, the enthusiast, the story, AND the downside math. Nothing hidden.

100% coverage. No item left without a value. No "TBD" or "see appraiser" in the master. The cross-reference spreadsheet is the artifact.
